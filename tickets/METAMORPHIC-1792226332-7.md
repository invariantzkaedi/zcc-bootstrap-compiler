## 5. Verification & Resolution Proof

- **Root Cause**: `csmith.h` synthetic stub in `part0_pp.c` previously declared `platform_main_end` as a function requiring `crc32_context` to be defined in scope. - **Resolution**:
  1. Defined `platform_main_begin()` and `platform_main_end(crc, flag)` as preprocessor macros expanding to empty in `part0_pp.c:zcc_stddef_text` matching `src/fake_csmith.h`.
  2. Defined `static uint32_t crc32_context;` in the synthetic header stub.
  3. Implemented ISO C99 §5.1.2.2.3 implicit `return 0;` for `main` fallthrough in `part4.c`.
