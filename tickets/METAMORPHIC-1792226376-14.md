## 5. Verification & Resolution Proof

- **Root Cause**:
  1. The Csmith harness previously lacked `-Isrc`, causing `fake_csmith.h` to fall back to the synthetic header stub in `part0_pp.c`, emitting a warning that was treated as a parser rejection.
  2. Delta reduction removed explicit `return 0;`, exposing missing ISO C99 §5.1.2.2.3 implicit return 0 for `main`.
- **Resolution**:
  1. Added `xorl %eax, %eax` before function epilogue for `main` falling off the end in `part4.c`.
  2. Defined `platform_main_begin()` and `platform_main_end(crc, flag)` macros, plus `static uint32_t crc32_context;` in `part0_pp.c` synthetic stub.
  3. Added `-Isrc` and filtered diagnostic warnings in `tools/metamorphic_csmith_gauntlet.py`.
- **Differential Verification**:
