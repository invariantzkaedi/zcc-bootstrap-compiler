fuzz(constant-folding): fix unsigned division, modulo, shifts, and comparisons in AST/IR folding

Goal:
Resolve remaining differential compiler mismatches in the 1000-program fuzzer gauntlet.

Outcome:
1000-program gauntlet passes successfully with identical checksum outputs to GCC.

Root cause:
- AST-level (part3.c, part4.c) and IR-level (ir_pass_manager.c) constant folding evaluated division, modulo, and comparisons as signed operations regardless of type unsigned status.
- ICP (icp_eval_expr) lacked truncation of lattice constant values before execution, causing upper sign-extend bits to contaminate logical shift and division results.

Patch summary:
- part3.c: Cast division/modulo operands to unsigned long long in eval_const_expr_raw and truncate lattice values to defined type widths in icp_eval_expr.
- ir_pass_manager.c: Implement truncate_ir_val helper and retrieve operand types in ir_pass_const_fold to perform type-aware integer divisions, modulos, right shifts, and comparisons.
- part4.c: Support modulo and apply unsigned division/modulo folding logic in eval_const_expr_p4_raw.
