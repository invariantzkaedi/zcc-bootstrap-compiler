# Influence Lexicon — 10 Functions

## 1. Delta (quantified change — the strongest influence move)
halves · 10x · eliminates · cuts · collapses · compresses ·
from-X-to-Y · zero-to-one · "was 12s, now 300ms"

## 2. First-mover (novelty, kept concrete)
first · only · introduces · debuts · unlocks · precedes ·
"no existing tool does" · "previously required N steps"

## 3. Reframe (perspective shift)
instead · unlike · inverts · flips · "turns X into Y" ·
"the real problem is" · "stop optimizing X, start measuring Y"

## 4. De-risk (influence through safety)
reversible · bounded · opt-in · gradual · fallback ·
escape-hatch · battle-tested · "worst case, you revert"

## 5. Friction-removal (influence through ease)
drop-in · zero-config · one-command · no-migration ·
backward-compatible · self-serve · works-out-of-the-box

## 6. Evidence (influence through authority)
measured · benchmarked · verified · reproduced · audited ·
cited · "in production since" · "n=1,000 runs"

## 7. Momentum (influence through motion)
shipped · landed · adopted-by · graduated · merged ·
"already powering" · "third team to switch"

## 8. Stakes (cost of inaction)
compounding · debt · exposure · window · deadline ·
"every week this costs" · "before the next incident"

## 9. Agency (influence through empowerment)
you-can-now · own · control · no-gatekeeper ·
"in your hands" · "without waiting on us"

## 10. Future-pull (vision, anchored)
"this makes possible" · path-to · next · beyond ·
"once this lands, X becomes trivial" · imagine-if (use once, max)
